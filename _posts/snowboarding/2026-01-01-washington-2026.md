It was a rough winter almost everywhere in Washington. A major windstorm and flooding in early December damaged a lot of mountain roads — and Leavenworth homes — and was followed by a disturbingly-warm, dry season. The North Cascades, however, were firing, so it was the perfect year for Jack to get his own sled!

  <h2>The Big Storm</h2>
  <div class="image-row">
    <a class="spotlight" href="https://images.mostlyrocks.com/snowboarding/2026/washington/11.jpeg"><img width=400 src="https://images.mostlyrocks.com/snowboarding/2026/washington/11.jpeg" /></a>
    <a class="spotlight" href="https://images.mostlyrocks.com/snowboarding/2026/washington/12.jpeg"><img width=400 src="https://images.mostlyrocks.com/snowboarding/2026/washington/12.jpeg" /></a>
</div>
  This storm was a generational tragedy. Homes, cars, roads, and trails were damaged so thoroughly that insurance is dropping our community en masse and, in lieu of a huge increase in USFS budget, recreating in the Cascades will be disrupted for the rest of my lifetime. We were out of power for a few days, which was scary as the house slowly got colder! Fortunately we had the fireplace and friends to make it through, and where there's a will there's a way for recreating.
